Selling your home is part financial decision, part emotional one. A clear plan
makes all the difference. Here's how I help Winnipeg homeowners sell smoothly —
and what to expect at each stage.

## Start with the right price

Pricing is the most important decision you'll make. I prepare a **comparative
market analysis (CMA)** using recent sales of similar nearby homes, current
competing listings, and your home's condition and features.

Pricing right from day one matters: well-priced homes attract more showings
early, when buyer interest is highest, and that competition tends to produce
stronger offers. Overpricing usually leads to a stale listing and, ironically, a
lower final number.

## Prepare your home to shine

You don't need a full renovation — just a thoughtful presentation. We'll walk
through your home together and I'll recommend only the changes likely to pay
off:

- declutter and depersonalize,
- deep clean and brighten,
- handle small, visible repairs, and
- consider light staging where it counts.

> The goal is simple: help buyers picture *their* life in your space.

## Market it everywhere buyers look

Great marketing combines professional photography, a compelling listing, broad
**MLS® exposure through the brokerage**, social media, and my personal network.
I present your home clearly in English, Punjabi, and Hindi to reach more
qualified buyers across Winnipeg's diverse communities.

## Review offers with a clear head

When offers arrive, I help you weigh more than just price — possession date,
conditions, deposit, and the strength of each buyer's financing all matter.
We'll choose the offer that best fits your goals and timeline, and I negotiate
hard on your behalf.

## From accepted offer to closing

Once you accept, the buyer typically works through their conditions (financing
and inspection). After those are satisfied, your lawyer handles the paperwork
and the sale completes on your possession date.

## What it costs to sell

Plan for the agreed real estate commission, legal fees, any mortgage payout or
discharge costs, and optional prep like staging. I'll give you a clear
**net-proceeds estimate** before we list, so you know exactly where you stand.
